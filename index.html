🏒 SickMitts VR

Clear your floor, put on your Quest 3, and tap below. Use the arrows on the floating panel to pick a game: Endless Object Treadmill sends objects down several lanes, Keep Away puts opposing defencemen on the ice around you, Insanity fills one wide lane with clutter that needs toe drags and fancy hands, Heads Up! sends a wiggling zig-zag training aid down the lane while a French bulldog fires cannonballs at your head, so you have to duck while you stickhandle, The Floor Is Lava turns the floor into lava with a winding path you have to keep your puck on, and Dog Attack fills a small patch of floor with wandering French bulldogs (or sharks, or T-rexes) you have to stickhandle around. Point a controller (or pinch with your hand) at the panel to adjust the settings, then start.
