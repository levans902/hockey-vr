🏒 SickMitts VR

Clear your floor, put on your Quest 3, and tap below. Use the arrows on the floating panel to pick a game: Endless Object Treadmill sends objects down several lanes, Keep Away puts opposing defencemen on the ice around you, and Insanity fills one wide lane with clutter that needs toe drags and fancy hands. Point a controller (or pinch with your hand) at the panel to adjust the settings, then start.
