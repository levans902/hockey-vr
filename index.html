🏒 SickMitts VR

Clear your floor, put on your Quest 3, and tap below. Pick a game on the floating panel: Endless Object Treadmill sends water bottles, pylons, dynamite, skates, gloves and mini nets sliding at your feet, and Keep Away puts opposing defencemen on the ice around you, skating, circling and attacking your puck. Point a controller (or pinch with your hand) at the panel to choose lanes, difficulty and speed, then start.
